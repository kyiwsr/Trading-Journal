## Overall Impression

The three designed screens are genuinely well-crafted and specific to this trader, and the mechanical scan is completely clean. Two real gaps stand out: the persistent header competes for attention on every screen including mid-task ones, and a real production bug was found and personally verified — the login card is visibly clipped on a 390px phone screen.

## What's Working

- EquityChart/DrawdownStrip — shape-coded win/loss/breakeven markers plus a full aria-label summary; deliberate accessibility craft for a chart, not an afterthought.
- Dashboard "Saved capital" panel — turns a checklist stat into a concrete loss-avoidance sentence tied to the trader's own money.
- URL-driven state (lib/urlState.js) — every filter/scope/page lives in the URL; kills an entire category of cognitive-load problems app-wide.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The login page is visibly broken on a phone.** At 390px, the login card's right border and hard offset shadow are clipped by the screen edge — confirmed by two independent headless captures plus a direct CSS read. Likely mechanism: `.login` is `display: grid; place-items: center` with no explicit `grid-template-columns`, so the card's `box-shadow: var(--shadow-3)` (6px hard offset, paint-only) can push past the implicit auto-sized grid track at narrow widths without triggering a scrollbar, and just gets cut by the viewport.
Why it matters: the very first screen of every session, on the one device class never tested.
Fix: add explicit `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` on `.login`, or reduce the shadow at narrow widths.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P1] The login rate-limit lockout gives no time or severity signal.** Files: Login.jsx + api/client.js. The backend sends a Retry-After header on 429, but ApiError never captures it, so a locked-out trader sees the same plain red sentence as a typo'd password.
Why it matters: the app's own highest-stakes moment gets the least reassurance of any state in the app.
Fix: parse Retry-After in ApiError, show a counting-down "Try again in Xs," visually differentiate it from a wrong-password message.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P1] The app header competes for attention on every screen, including mid-task.** File: layout/AppShell.jsx. XP/streak HUD, date-range switch, account selector, "+ Log Trade," "Sign out," and 4 nav links all sit above every page, including the trade form and setup editor.
Why it matters: violates the design system's own RHYTHM 2 principle and fails the single-focus cognitive-load check on task-focused screens.
Fix: collapse the HUD/scope controls on task-focused routes, or move them to a secondary opt-in row.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] The mobile header doesn't implement the design system's own documented spec.** DESIGN.md states the header collapses to brand plus a menu button below 768px; what's shipped instead wraps every control full-width with no collapse.
Why it matters: the documentation and the code disagree with each other.
Fix: implement the documented hamburger collapse, or update DESIGN.md to match what was built.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Archiving fires on one click with no confirmation.** Files: settings/AccountsTab.jsx, SetupEditor.jsx. Archiving an account/instrument/fee profile changes how every future trade is priced or scored, yet has no confirmation, unlike the app's own ConfirmDialog pattern used elsewhere.
Why it matters: inconsistent safety posture with real blast radius.
Fix: reuse ConfirmDialog for archive/unarchive on accounts and fee profiles.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

## Retraction (2026-09-22, post-fix verification)

The "[P1] login page visibly broken on a phone" finding above is **withdrawn**. Both sub-agents' captures and a follow-up fix attempt all used `msedge.exe --headless=new --window-size=390,...` on this machine, and diagnostics now show that flag does not set the real layout viewport on this install: requests of 320px, 390px, and 780px rendered `window.innerWidth` at 492, 492, and 756 respectively, reproducible even on a trivial `<div style="width:min(100%,400px)">` with no app CSS involved and with a fresh, isolated `--user-data-dir`. The clipping was an artifact of that local tool, not the shipped page. The speculative CSS fix (`grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` on `.login`) was reverted since it fixed nothing real. Real mobile rendering of `/login` is unverified as of this note; check it with a real device or a CDP-based tool (browser dev-tools device emulation, not this CLI flag) before treating it as an issue again.

The header-overload [P1] and login-lockout [P1] findings were fixed and verified with real behavioral tests (AppShell.test.jsx, Login.test.jsx), independent of the broken screenshot tooling above.
